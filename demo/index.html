Photosensitivity warning

This picture flickers at the plugin's own defaults. A Baird televisor lit its picture one point at a time, once a picture, and so does this. At the default Persistence of 0.1 s every lit point swings by about half its light 12½ times a second, so on a bright picture most of the field flickers in saturated red past WCAG's red-flash threshold: 70% of the worst ten-degree window on white, 64% on Resolume's brightest demo clip, 50% on the plugin's test card, 33% on a flat 25% grey, 5–10% on dark clips (WCAG fails 25%). Flicker like this can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy. If you, or anyone who can see your screen, may be affected, do not start it.

Frame Fill, a higher Lamp, a Picture Rate near 5 or 8, Persistence 0.05 s or less and Sync Free make it worse. Turning Persistence up calms it: 0.2 s or more keeps the brightest demo clip under the line, 0.6 s or more keeps white under, and at 1 s nothing measured crosses except a Picture Rate of 4 to 7. These are the plugin's own measurements (its README, Flashing); this page measures nothing.

Nothing is drawn until you press Start, whatever link brought you here. The line under the picture says when a setting is in the range that flickers; Pause, under the picture, stops it at once.
