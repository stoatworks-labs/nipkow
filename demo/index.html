Photosensitivity warning

Some settings make this picture flicker. Turned down, Persistence at 0.5 s or less on a white source (0.15 s or less on bright clips), or a Picture Rate of 4 to 7, makes the whole picture flicker in saturated red more than three times a second, which can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy. Frame Fill spreads whatever flickers over the whole frame.

The page starts at the plugin's own defaults (a link that carries other settings asks first), and at those (Persistence 1 s, a flash-safety default) nothing crosses WCAG's flash thresholds. If you, or anyone who can see your screen, may be affected, leave Persistence and Picture Rate where they are. The line under the picture warns when a setting is in or near that range; Defaults, above the controls, puts every control back, and Pause, under the picture, stops it at once.
